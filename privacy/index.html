Warlord.OS legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers the Warlord.OS and Saiyan Strike apps, including the Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok apps that they use, and this website. They are publishing tools run by one person, Tom Brownhill, trading as Warlord.OS, in the United Kingdom. They post content to social media accounts that Tom Brownhill owns or manages, such as BallinGodz.

1. What information the apps handle

  • Sign-in tokens and account IDs for the owner's own Facebook Pages, Instagram, X and TikTok accounts. These let the apps publish posts and videos.
  • The content being published, such as text, images and video.
  • Public performance numbers for the owner's own posts, such as views, likes and comments counts, so the owner can see how posts are doing.
  • Basic logs such as timestamps and error messages, used to fix problems.

The apps do not collect personal information from other people's accounts, do not sell any information and do not use it for advertising.

2. Where it is stored

Everything above is stored on the owner's own Windows computer. Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted. The information is not uploaded to any other server, except that content and tokens are sent to Meta, X or TikTok when the owner publishes a post. Those platforms handle it under their own privacy policies.

3. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, forms, cookies or trackers. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

4. How long information is kept

Sign-in tokens are kept until the connection is removed or the token expires. Logs and performance numbers are kept only as long as they are useful to run the apps, and are then deleted.

5. Deleting your data

The apps do not store information about other people. If you have connected one of these apps to your own Facebook account, you can remove it at any time, which deletes its access. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites and remove the app.

6. Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information about you, or object to how it is used. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
